# STEAM MARKETING PACKAGE

## 🎯 Store Page Copy

### Game Title
**ZOMBIE SURVIVAL** (working title — suggest: "Deadlands: Survival", "Last Stand", "Wasteland Z")

### Short Description (max 300 chars)
Sandbox survival in a zombie-infested open world. Build bases, scavenge wrecks for vehicle parts, fight hordes at night. PvP raiding, co-op, and endless crafting. How long can you survive?

### About This Game
```
THE WORLD ENDED. YOU DIDN'T.

A deadly virus has turned the population into ravenous undead. 
Scavenge abandoned cities, build fortified bases, and assemble 
vehicles from scrap. But beware — when night falls, the horde awakens.

CORE FEATURES:

🧟 DYNAMIC ZOMBIE AI
- Walkers roam by day, but at night they become faster, stronger, 
  and hunt in coordinated hordes of 50+.
- Special infected: Runners, Tanks, Spitters, and terrifying Bosses.

🏗️ BASE BUILDING & RAIDING
- Claim your territory with a Claim Block and build anything from 
  a wooden shack to an armored fortress.
- Raid enemy bases with explosives — but only during night hours!

🚗 VEHICLE ASSEMBLY (Hurtworld-style)
- Find wrecked vehicles across the map and scavenge parts to 
  rebuild them. Engine, wheels, battery — every part matters.

🌙 DAY/NIGHT CYCLE
- 45 real minutes = 24 in-game hours. Day is for scavenging, 
  night is for surviving. The horde comes at sundown.

🎮 MULTIPLAYER
- Play solo, co-op with friends, or join 50-player PvP servers.
- Dedicated server support with full admin tools.

🔧 DEEP CRAFTING
- 100+ craftable items. Research blueprints, upgrade workbenches, 
  unlock endgame tech.

EARLY ACCESS ROADMAP:
- Q1 2027: EA Launch (4 biomes, 5 vehicles, 20 weapons)
- Q2 2027: Helicopters, electricity, auto-turrets
- Q3 2027: New map expansion, faction wars
- Q4 2027: Full release
```
